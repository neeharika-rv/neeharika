    Twelve thousand files, found

    Boeing's knowledge lived in old Windows file shares, where finding anything meant browsing folder by folder. On a team of five, we spent twelve months building it a new home in the cloud on AWS GovCloud. I owned two parts: moving the files, and making them answer questions.

    I designed a serverless pipeline that carried more than 12,000 files, many over 10 GB, into Amazon S3, keeping every file's permissions and security markings intact. Then I built SmartSearch, which reads inside documents and returns results in under ten seconds, and a Bedrock chatbot that answers questions straight from the documents. I also built the APIs underneath and brought response times down from seconds to milliseconds.

    impact7M+files and documents, used by 12,000+ people
    toolsAWS GovCloud, Step Functions, Lambda, DataSync, DynamoDB, OpenSearch, Bedrock

    Flour & books

    Away from the keyboard, I bake and I read. They're more alike than they look. A recipe is a little program you run with your hands, and a library is the best search engine ever made.

    Both taught me habits I bring to work: read everything before you start, measure instead of guessing, and taste before you serve.

    How I work

    Method

    1. Read the whole recipe first. Understand the data, the people and the rules before writing code.
    2. Weigh, don't guess. Decide what "better" means and how to measure it.
    3. Let it rise. Build small, test, give it time.
    4. Taste before serving. Ship with dashboards so you know how it's doing.
